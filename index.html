Privacy Policy – Zikr Counter

Last updated: 5-11-2026

Zikr Counter ("the app") is a tally counter for dhikr and tasbih. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information stored on your device

Your counters, dhikr names, colours, targets and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers, we do not collect this data, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions the app uses

  • Display over other apps: to show the floating counter and the black-screen mode.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to keep the floating counter running and show the count in a notification.
  • Vibration: for tap feedback.
  • Run at startup: to restore the floating counter after the phone restarts.

These permissions are used only for the features described. They are not used to collect personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell your personal data. Apart from the advertising described above, the app does not send data to any server.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
